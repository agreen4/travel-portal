**Before You Go**
- Download the Tokyo Disney Resort Official App before leaving Champaign
- Create an account and link your DisneySea reservation in the app
- Pre-load tickets in the app at the hotel — park gate WiFi is patchy
- App handles: Disney Premier Access purchases, mobile food orders, wait times, maps

**Getting There**
Leave &Here Tokyo Ueno by **7:00am**. Take the **JR Keiyo Line** from Keiyo/Shinonome (or transfer at Tokyo Station) → **Maihama Station** (~40–45 min). **Disney Resort Line monorail** → Tokyo DisneySea Station. Aim to be at the gates by **8:00am** — park opens 9am; Fantasy Springs queue starts forming early.

---

**Morning — Fantasy Springs first**
Fantasy Springs is a ~20-minute walk from the main entrance and the highest-demand area in the park. Head there at rope drop.

As soon as you're inside: buy Disney Premier Access (DPA) for **Anna and Elsa's Frozen Journey** immediately — this sells out within minutes. DPA is ~¥2,000/person/attraction, capped at one purchase per hour:
- On entry → DPA: **Frozen**
- 1 hour later → DPA: **Rapunzel's Lantern Festival**
- 1 hour later → DPA: **Peter Pan's Never Land Adventure**

Priority order inside Fantasy Springs:
1. **Anna and Elsa's Frozen Journey** — highest demand; waits exceed 120 min by 10am without DPA
2. **Rapunzel's Lantern Festival** — second most popular
3. **Peter Pan's Never Land Adventure** — DPA lasts longest
4. **Fairy Tinker Bell's Busy Buggies** — gentle ride; low wait, good for any time

**Mid-morning — low-wait rides while DPA recharges**
- **Sinbad's Storybook Voyage** — gentle boat dark ride, a DisneySea classic
- **Fortress Explorations** — walk-through interactive ship; not a ride, no queue, endlessly explorable
- **Nemo & Friends SeaRider** — gentle submarine ride
- **20,000 Leagues Under the Sea** — DisneySea-exclusive submarine dark ride

**Afternoon — thrill rides and flex time**
- **Indiana Jones Adventure** — bumpy jeep ride, thrilling but accessible; shorter waits mid-afternoon
- **Toy Story Mania** — carnival-style shooting game
- **Journey to the Center of the Earth** — iconic launch coaster inside a volcano

**Dining**
Eat at **11am or 2pm** — noon is peak crowd surge everywhere.
- **Royal Banquet of Arendelle** (Fantasy Springs) — themed Frozen restaurant; requires mobile order and you must already be inside Fantasy Springs
- **Magellan's** (near Fortress Explorations) — DisneySea's most atmospheric sit-down; book via app in advance
- **Vulcania** (inside Mount Prometheus) — best quick-service atmosphere; right next to Journey to the Center of the Earth

Use mobile ordering in the app while waiting in standby lines.

**Getting Back**
Leave the park by **7:00–7:30pm**. Board the **Disney Resort Line monorail** back to **Maihama Station**, then take the **JR Keiyo Line** from Maihama to **Tokyo Station** (~20 min). Transfer to the **JR Yamanote Line** northbound to **Ueno** (~15 min). Back at hotel by ~8:30pm.

**Evening — Convenience Store Wind-Down**
A convenience store haul is the perfect low-key ending after a full Disney day. Stop at the **7-Eleven or Lawson** near the hotel: cold **Asahi or Kirin beer**, a box of **Oni Killer sake** (single-serve juice box format), **Jagabee potato sticks** (shoyu butter flavor), a **Black Thunder chocolate bar**, seasonal **Pocky**, and a fresh sandwich (egg salad or katsu). If there's ice cream in a limited seasonal flavor, get it. This is Japan convenience store culture at its finest.
