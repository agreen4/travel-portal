Arrive Hong Kong at **9:00pm** on CX 801. Clear customs, then take the **Airport Express** to **Kowloon Station** (~24 min, HK$90/person). From Kowloon Station, board the **free K3 shuttle bus** — Hotel ICON is a designated stop, buses run every 12 minutes. Check in around 10:30pm.

> The K3 shuttle is free with your Airport Express ticket stub. If bags are heavy and the shuttle feels slow, a taxi from the airport direct to Hotel ICON is ~HK$280 + HK$5/bag.

Despite the late hour, take a short 10-minute walk to **Kai Kai Dessert** (113–115 Parkes Street, Jordan) — open until 1am. Order the **black sesame soup** and **tofu skin with ginkgo**. A Michelin Bib Gourmand dessert stall that started as a street cart in 1979, moved to Parkes Street in 2025. A perfect, effortless first taste of Hong Kong. Early night.
