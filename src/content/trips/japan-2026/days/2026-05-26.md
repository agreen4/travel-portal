Arrive Hong Kong at **9:00pm** on CX 801. Clear customs, then take the **Airport Express** to **[Kowloon Station](https://maps.app.goo.gl/yfgtg5fxmZK9XtUE8)** (~24 min, HK$90/person). [Hotel ICON](https://maps.app.goo.gl/UTjbw3pyRj6jsvJ6A), buses run every 12 minutes. Check in around 10:30pm.

> If bags are heavy and the shuttle feels slow, a taxi from the airport direct to Hotel ICON is ~HK$280 + HK$5/bag.

Once we arrive, we'll, take a short 10-minute walk to **Kai Kai Dessert** ([113–115 Parkes Street, Jordan](https://maps.app.goo.gl/f2VvzPytJwbgAFYL8)) — open until 1am. Order the **black sesame soup** and **tofu skin with ginkgo**. A Michelin Bib Gourmand dessert stall that started as a street cart in 1979 and moved to Parkes Street in 2025.
