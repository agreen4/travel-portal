> Set alarm for **5:15 AM** — photography priority day.

**5:30–8:00 AM — Dawn Shoot**
**Patkuli** (Rahukohtu tn 5) — panoramic Lower Town + harbor. **Kohtuotsa** (Kohtu tn 12, 200m away) — complementary angle + "Tallinn" sign. Descend via **Pikk jalg (Long Leg Street)** — the most photogenic street in the city.

**8:30 AM** — Breakfast at Hotel Telegraaf.

**10:00 AM–1:00 PM — Lower Old Town**
**Müürivahe** (medieval wall + sweater market); **Town Hall Square** (Gothic details, iron work, guild signs); **St. Olaf's Church tower** — elevated rooftop view (€5).

**1:00 PM** — Light lunch; Old Town café or bakery on Pikk Street.

**2:00–5:00 PM — Kalamaja Neighborhood**
20 min walk/Bolt northwest: pastel wooden houses, café culture; Kalma Street. **Lennusadam** (Seaplane Harbour Museum) — spectacular industrial hangar interior. One of the most impressive museum spaces in the Baltic.

**6:00 PM — Dinner: Noa Chef's Hall ⭐**
Rannamõisa tee 3; Bolt ~10 min (~€8). Michelin-starred restaurant on a sea cliff setting with Baltic evening light. Allow 3 hours.
