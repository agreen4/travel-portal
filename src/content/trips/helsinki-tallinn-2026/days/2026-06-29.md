**Morning** — Final Tallinn hour; check out of Telegraaf; Bolt to D-Terminal.

**7:30 AM** — Depart TLL on Tallink ferry. Arrive Helsinki West Harbour **9:30am**. Bolt to Scandic Grand Central (~€15–20 from West Harbour); drop bags.

**~Mid-Morning — Suomenlinna Sea Fortress**
Walk to **Market Square pier**, HSL ferry every 15–20 min (~€3.20 day ticket); 15 min crossing. Allow **2.5–3 hours**: King's Gate, cannon batteries + ramparts, clifftop path at **Pikku Mustasaari** (best Helsinki skyline view looking back at the cathedral), Submarine Vesikko (optional).

**~1:45 PM — Lunch: Old Market Hall**
2 min walk from the Suomenlinna ferry landing at Market Square. Salmon soup, Baltic herring, Finnish cheese, cloudberry. Open Mon–Sat; walk-in.

**3:00–5:30 PM — Senate Square & Harbor**
**Helsinki Cathedral**, **Senate Square**, **Uspenski Cathedral** (red-brick Orthodox; view the exterior from the harbor inlet).

**8:00 PM — Dinner: Restaurant MoMo**
Nepalese dumplings. Walk-in; arrive early as it fills fast.
