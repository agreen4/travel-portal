**Morning** — Breakfast at Klaus K. Bolt to Länsiterminaali (West Harbour) by ~9:45am. Arrive at terminal 30–45 minutes before departure.

**10:30 AM** — Depart HEL on Tallink ferry. Spend time on deck — the Finnish archipelago receding, the Estonian coast approaching. The crossing takes 2 hours.

**12:30 PM** — Arrive D-Terminal, Tallinn. Bolt to Old Town (~€6). Check in to Hotel Telegraaf (Vene 9).

**~2:00–5:00 PM** — First Old Town walk: get oriented, note photography positions for dawn. **Town Hall Square**, **Müürivahe** (medieval wall street), **Viru Gates**. Pre-dinner cocktail at the Telegraaf bar.

**6:00 PM** — Dinner at **Rataskaevu 16** (Rataskaevu tn 16) — elk, dark rye bread, Estonian flavors; warm candlelit room. Allow 2 hours.

**10:00–10:30 PM** — Walk to **Patkuli** or **Kohtuotsa** viewpoint — glowing Nordic sky, nearly empty streets.
