**8:00 AM** — Slower start; breakfast at Telegraaf; final Old Town photography in morning light.

**10:30 AM — Telliskivi Creative City**
20 min walk/Bolt west: converted industrial buildings, street art, design shops, murals along **Telliskivi tänav**. One of Tallinn's most vibrant neighborhoods.

**12:30 PM — Lunch: Balti Jaama Turg (Baltic Station Market)**
Estonian black bread, fresh produce, local dairy, gua bao at Baojaam. The most local food experience of the Tallinn stay.

**2:30–5:00 PM — Afternoon**
Options: rest and edit photos at the hotel, or **Kadriorg Park** (30 min walk east) — baroque palace park and KUMU Art Museum.

**6:00 PM** — Pre-dinner cocktail at Telegraaf bar or Town Hall Square terrace.

**7:30 PM — Dinner: F-Hoone**
Telliskivi 60a. Casual, industrial-chic. Eclectic European menu, great beer. Walk-in.

**After dinner** — Final Tallinn night walk: quiet lanes, glowing 10pm Nordic sky, stone walls in warm light.
